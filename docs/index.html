Self-Hosted Management for Gaming Clubs & Esports Venues

Stop paying per-PC monthly fees to cloud software that stops working when your internet drops. Run sessions, night packs, player balances, staff RBAC, and Windows kiosk PCs locally on your own LAN.

Billing Engine

Tariffs, Packages & Night Packs

Zone-based hourly rates (Standard / VIP / Bootcamp), weekday vs weekend time-of-day schedules, fixed-hour bundles, night packs, session pause/resume, and seat transfers.

Security & Anti-Fraud

Staff RBAC, Ledger & Audit Trail

Separate Owner, Manager, Cashier, and Tech roles with concurrency-safe real + bonus balances, cashback, refunds, and a tamper-evident audit log (SHA-256 hash chain).

Cash Control

Bar POS, Shifts & Z-Reports

Touch-friendly bar checkout with stock tracking, shifts with an opening float and a blind cash count, expected vs counted variance, printable Z-reports and CSV exports for accounting.

Windows Client

Fail-Closed Kiosk Shell & LAN Agent

Dedicated WPF lock-screen shell blocking system shortcuts plus a Windows Service agent with LAN server auto-discovery and scripted PowerShell deployment.

Blackout-Resilient

100% On-Prem Docker Deployment

Single docker-compose stack runs right inside your club server. If external internet drops, your cashiers and gaming PCs keep working with zero interruption.
